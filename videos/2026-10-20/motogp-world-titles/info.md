# Who has won the most MotoGP world titles?

## Description
Leslie Graham won the first 500cc World Championship in 1949 on an AJS. Seventy-seven seasons later, who has won the most premier-class world titles?

This race tracks every 500cc and MotoGP world champion from 1949 to 2025, with each bar in the colours of the bike the rider won his latest title on. Geoff Duke and John Surtees set the early pace, Mike Hailwood takes four in a row for MV Agusta, then Giacomo Agostini wins seven straight and an eighth on a Yamaha in 1975. Mick Doohan's five Repsol Hondas, Valentino Rossi's seven and Marc Márquez's comeback title for Ducati in 2025 bring two riders level on seven, one behind Ago.

For European fans: Italy and Spain own the top three, with Agostini and Rossi for Italy and Márquez for Spain, and Britain's Duke, Surtees and Hailwood all won four.

Key Highlights:
Ago's Record: Giacomo Agostini won seven titles in a row for MV Agusta (1966–72) and an eighth with Yamaha in 1975.
Doohan's Five: Mick Doohan won five straight titles for Honda from 1994 to 1998.
Rossi's Seven: Valentino Rossi won titles on both Honda and Yamaha between 2001 and 2009.
Márquez Matches Rossi: Marc Márquez's 2025 title, his first on a Ducati and six years after his last, drew him level with Rossi on seven.

Data includes: Giacomo Agostini, Valentino Rossi, Marc Márquez, Mick Doohan, Geoff Duke, John Surtees, Mike Hailwood, Eddie Lawson, Kenny Roberts, Wayne Rainey, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Wikipedia, List of 500cc/MotoGP World Riders' Champions (1949–2025); Crash.net, All MotoGP world champions from 1949 to the present day

## Colours
Each bar takes the colours of the team or bike the rider won his most recent title with, changing when he wins on a new one (like the F1 drivers' titles video):
- 1950s–70s works bikes: AJS black and gold, Norton silver, Gilera deep red, MV Agusta red and silver; Agostini switches to Yamaha white and red in 1975.
- Kenny Roberts: Yamaha yellow and black. Lawson and Rainey: Marlboro Yamaha red and white; Lawson switches to Rothmans Honda blue for his 1989 title. Spencer: HRC Honda 1983, then Rothmans Honda 1985. Gardner: Rothmans Honda. Schwantz: Lucky Strike Suzuki.
- Doohan: HRC Honda 1994, then Repsol Honda orange from 1995. Crivillé, Hayden and Márquez (2013–19) also Repsol Honda; Stoner Ducati red 2007, then Repsol Honda 2011.
- Rossi: Nastro Azzurro Honda 2001, Repsol Honda 2002, then Yamaha blue from 2004. Lorenzo: Yamaha blue. Quartararo: Monster Energy Yamaha. Mir: Suzuki Ecstar blue. Bagnaia and Márquez 2025: Ducati red. Martín 2024: Prima Pramac purple.
- Where a team's livery was mixed or I couldn't confirm it, the manufacturer's colours are used (Suzuki blue for Lucchinelli 1981, Uncini 1982 and Roberts Jr. 2000; Yamaha blue for the 2008–10 Fiat Yamaha bikes, which were white, blue and red).
- Riders are hidden until their first title.
