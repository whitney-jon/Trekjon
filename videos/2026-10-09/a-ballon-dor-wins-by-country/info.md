# Most Ballon d'Or Wins by Country (1956–2025)

## Title
Most Ballon d'Or Wins by Country 1956–2025

## Description
Which country has produced the most Ballon d'Or winners? England and Spain lead early, then Germany and the Netherlands trade places for decades before Messi carries Argentina to 8, and Dembélé's 2025 win pulls France level on 8. Covers every men's Ballon d'Or from 1956 to 2025 (not awarded in 2020), counted by the winner's nationality. Source: Ballon d'Or winners lists from Sports Illustrated and Topend Sports.

## Data notes
- Cumulative wins at each odd year 1959–2025 (34 time points); every value counted from the full year-by-year winners list.
- Germany includes West Germany (Müller, Beckenbauer, Rummenigge). Alfredo Di Stéfano (1957, 1959) is counted for Spain and Omar Sívori (1961) for Italy, as in Topend Sports' by-nationality table. Czechoslovakia (Masopust) and Czechia (Nedvěd) are kept separate.
- Final top 10: Argentina 8, France 8, Portugal 7, Germany 7, Netherlands 7, Italy 5, Brazil 5, England 5, Spain 4, Soviet Union 3.
- Flags were not available for this run, so country code chips are shown instead.
