# Most Super Bowl Wins by Team (1967–2026)

## Title
Most Super Bowl Wins by Team 1967–2026

## Description
Every Super Bowl champion from Super Bowl I in 1967 to the Seahawks' Super Bowl LX win in February 2026. The Packers lead early, the Steelers take over in the 1970s, the 49ers and Cowboys reach five in the 1990s, and the Steelers and Patriots finish level on six. Source: Super Bowl results from FOX Sports and Flashscore, checked against Goal.com.
#NFL #SuperBowl #Patriots #Steelers #Football

## Data notes
- Cumulative wins at each odd year 1967–2025 plus 2026 (31 time points); every value counted from the full game-by-game winners list.
- Wins are counted by franchise under its current name: Colts include the Baltimore Colts (Super Bowl V); Raiders include Oakland and Los Angeles; Rams include St. Louis; Commanders include the Washington Redskins era.
- Final top 10: Steelers 6, Patriots 6, 49ers 5, Cowboys 5, Chiefs 4, Giants 4, Packers 4, Broncos 3, Commanders 3, Raiders 3.
