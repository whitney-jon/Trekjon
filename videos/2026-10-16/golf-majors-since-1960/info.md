# Who has won the most golf majors since 1960?

## Description
In 1960 Arnold Palmer won the Masters and the US Open and set off golf's modern era. Since then, 267 men's majors have been played. Who has won the most of them?

This race tracks every major championship win (the Masters, the US Open, the Open and the PGA Championship) from 1960 to 2026. Palmer leads early, Jack Nicklaus takes over in 1967 and finishes on 18, and Tiger Woods charges from 1997 to reach 15. Rory McIlroy's back-to-back Masters wins in 2025 and 2026 lift him into the top 9 in the final year.

For European fans: Nick Faldo (6 majors), Seve Ballesteros (5) and Rory McIlroy (6) all reach the top ten, and McIlroy now sits level with Palmer, Trevino, Faldo and Mickelson.

Key Highlights:
The King's Start: Arnold Palmer won six majors from 1960 to 1964 and led until Nicklaus passed him in 1967.
The Golden Bear: Jack Nicklaus won his 18th major at the 1986 Masters, aged 46.
Tiger's Charge: Tiger Woods won 14 majors from 1997 to 2008 and his 15th at the 2019 Masters.
Rory Joins the Six Club: McIlroy's 2026 Masters was his sixth major, level with Palmer, Trevino, Faldo and Mickelson.

Data includes: Jack Nicklaus, Tiger Woods, Gary Player, Tom Watson, Arnold Palmer, Lee Trevino, Nick Faldo, Phil Mickelson, Rory McIlroy, Seve Ballesteros, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Wikipedia, Chronological list of men's major golf champions (1960–2026); The Golf News Net, men's major winners by year

## Scope
Majors won from 1960 onwards (everyone starts at 0 in 1960). All-time totals including earlier wins put pre-1960 players (Hagen 11, Hogan 9, Vardon, Jones, Sarazen 7) in the top ten and push McIlroy, Mickelson and Faldo out, so the 1960 start is stated in the title and question. The 2020 Open was not played (COVID-19).
