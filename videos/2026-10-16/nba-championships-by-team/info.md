# Which team has won the most NBA championships?

## Description
In 1947 the Philadelphia Warriors won the first BAA title, the league that became the NBA. Eighty seasons later, after the New York Knicks won their first championship since 1973 this June, which team has won the most NBA championships?

This race tracks every franchise's BAA and NBA titles from 1947 to 2026. The Minneapolis Lakers ruled the early years with five titles, Bill Russell's Celtics won eight in a row from 1959 to 1966, and the Lakers chased Boston for decades until they drew level on 17 in 2020.

For European fans: European stars have been central to recent titles, with Dirk Nowitzki leading Dallas to the 2011 title and Nikola Jokić leading Denver to the 2023 title.

Key Highlights:
The Minneapolis Dynasty: The Lakers won five of the first eight NBA-era titles in Minneapolis (1949–1954).
Eight in a Row: The Celtics won every title from 1959 to 1966, still the longest streak in league history.
Level at Last: The Lakers' 2020 title tied the Celtics on 17 championships.
Boston Back in Front: The Celtics' 2024 title made it 18, the most of any franchise.

Data includes: Boston Celtics, Los Angeles Lakers, Golden State Warriors, Chicago Bulls, San Antonio Spurs, Philadelphia 76ers, Detroit Pistons, Miami Heat, New York Knicks, Milwaukee Bucks, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: List of BAA/NBA champions 1947–2024 (Soap Central NBA Finals list; The Analyst, titles by franchise); 2025 and 2026 Finals (Al Jazeera; Wikipedia, 2026 NBA Finals)

## How names and logos work
Each franchise's titles are counted together. A bar shows the name and logo the team won its titles under, and switches when the franchise first wins under a new identity: Minneapolis Lakers to Lakers (1972), Philadelphia Warriors to Warriors (1975), Syracuse Nationals to 76ers (1967), Seattle SuperSonics to Thunder (2025). Teams are hidden until their first title. The Baltimore Bullets (1948 champions) folded in 1954 and are a separate club from the later Bullets/Wizards.
