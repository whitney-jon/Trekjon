# Which country has won the most men's Grand Slam titles?

## Description
When tennis went open in 1968, Australia ruled the sport through Rod Laver, Ken Rosewall and John Newcombe. Since then, 234 men's Grand Slam singles titles have been won. Which country has won the most?

This race tracks every men's Grand Slam singles title by the champion's country, from the 1968 French Open to the 2026 US Open. Australia leads until the USA's golden era of Jimmy Connors, John McEnroe, Pete Sampras and Andre Agassi takes over in 1981. Then Sweden, Switzerland and Serbia climb through Björn Borg, Roger Federer and Novak Djokovic, and Rafael Nadal and Carlos Alcaraz carry Spain to second.

For European fans: seven of the top nine are European nations, led by Spain (36), Sweden (25), Serbia (24) and Switzerland (23), with Italy rising fast through Jannik Sinner.

Key Highlights:
The Aussie Start: Australia led the Open Era count from 1968 until 1980, through Laver, Rosewall and Newcombe.
American Era: The USA took the lead in 1981 and has won 52 titles, with Sampras (14) the top American.
Spain's Rise: Nadal's 2019 US Open title moved Spain past Sweden into second; Spain now has 36.
2026 So Far: Alcaraz (Australian Open), Alexander Zverev (French Open and US Open) and Sinner (Wimbledon) shared this year's four titles.

Data includes: United States, Spain, Sweden, Serbia, Switzerland, Australia, Czechoslovakia, Argentina, Italy, Russia, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Wikipedia, List of Grand Slam men's singles champions (1968–2026); Reuters (2026 US Open), Drishti IAS (2026 Wimbledon)

## Rules
- Open Era only: from the 1968 French Open (the 1968 Australian Open was amateur). 1977 had two Australian Opens, 1986 none, and the 2020 Wimbledon was cancelled: 234 titles in all.
- Country = the nation the champion represented when he won. Historical nations are their own rows: Czechoslovakia (Kodeš 3, Lendl 8; Lendl became a US citizen in 1992, after his last major) is separate from the Czech Republic (Korda 1998); West Germany (Becker 1985–89, 4) is separate from Germany (Becker 1991 and 1996, Stich 1991, Zverev 2026 ×2). Johan Kriek: South Africa for 1981, USA for the Dec 1982 Australian Open (naturalised Aug 1982).
- Bar colours: national colours, adjusted so neighbours don't match (Australia green and gold, Serbia white, Italy deep blue).
