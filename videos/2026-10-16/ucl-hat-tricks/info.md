# Who has scored the most Champions League hat-tricks?

## Description
The first hat-trick of the Champions League era came from PSV's Juul Ellerman in September 1992. Since then, more than 170 have been scored. Who has scored the most Champions League hat-tricks?

This race tracks every Champions League hat-trick by player from the 1992-93 season to October 2026. Filippo Inzaghi set the early pace with three, then Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo traded hat-tricks through the 2010s until both reached eight, with Robert Lewandowski and Kylian Mbappé chasing.

For European fans: the race is full of Europe's great strikers, from Marco van Basten and Andriy Shevchenko to Lewandowski (6), Mbappé (5) and Karim Benzema (4).

Key Highlights:
The Early Leader: Filippo Inzaghi was the first player to three Champions League hat-tricks (Juventus 1998 and 2000, Milan 2002).
Messi's Big Nights: Messi scored four against Arsenal in 2010 and five against Leverkusen in 2012.
Level at the Top: Ronaldo's hat-trick against Atlético Madrid in March 2019 drew him level with Messi on eight.
The Chasers: Lewandowski has six and Mbappé five, after Mbappé's three hat-tricks in 2025.

Data includes: Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Robert Lewandowski, Kylian Mbappé, Karim Benzema, Filippo Inzaghi, Mario Gómez, Luiz Adriano, Neymar, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Wikipedia, List of UEFA Champions League hat-tricks (1992–2026); UEFA.com, Champions League hat-tricks record list (Sep 2026) and top scorers (14 Oct 2026)
