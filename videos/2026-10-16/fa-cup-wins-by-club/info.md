# Which club has won the most FA Cups?

## Description
In 1946 the FA Cup returned after the war with Aston Villa and Blackburn Rovers level on six wins each, and the Victorian side Wanderers still on five. Eighty years later, which club has won the most FA Cups?

This race tracks every club's all-time FA Cup wins (counting every final since 1872), year by year from 1946 to 2026. Tottenham's golden run ended Villa's long reign in 1991, Manchester United's 1990s Doubles took them clear in 1996, and Arsenal's three wins in four years (2014, 2015, 2017) finally put them top.

For European football fans: the FA Cup is the oldest national cup competition in the world, first played in 1872. Manchester United (13) and Arsenal (14) have now built a gap of five or more wins over everyone else.

Key Highlights:
The Villa Era: Aston Villa led the all-time list from 1946 until 1991, sitting on seven wins after their 1957 final.
Spurs Take Over: Tottenham's eighth win in 1991 set a new record.
United's Turn: Manchester United's 1996 win was their ninth, taking the outright lead.
Arsenal on Top: Arsenal's 12th win in 2015 made them the record holders, and they now have 14.

Data includes: Arsenal, Manchester United, Tottenham Hotspur, Chelsea, Liverpool, Manchester City, Aston Villa, Blackburn Rovers, Newcastle United, Wanderers, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Wikipedia, List of FA Cup finals (1872–2026); Sports Illustrated, FA Cup winners complete list (2026)
