# Who has won the most F1 world championships?

## Description
Giuseppe Farina won the first Formula 1 World Championship in 1950 for Alfa Romeo. Seventy-six seasons later, who has won the most F1 drivers' titles?

This race tracks every World Drivers' Championship from 1950 to 2025. Juan Manuel Fangio's five titles stood as the record for 46 years, until Michael Schumacher's Ferrari run took him to seven. Lewis Hamilton matched him in 2020, and Sebastian Vettel and Max Verstappen joined Alain Prost on four.

For European fans: Europeans dominate the list, from Schumacher and Vettel (Germany) and Prost (France) to Hamilton, Jackie Stewart and Jim Clark (Great Britain) and Verstappen (Netherlands). Each bar wears the colours of the car the driver won his latest title in.

Key Highlights:
Fangio's Record: Juan Manuel Fangio won five titles with four different teams between 1951 and 1957.
Schumacher's Seven: Michael Schumacher's sixth title in 2003 broke Fangio's record, and he won a seventh in 2004.
Hamilton Matches: Lewis Hamilton's seventh title in 2020 drew him level with Schumacher.
The Four-Time Club: Alain Prost, Sebastian Vettel and Max Verstappen have four titles each.

Data includes: Michael Schumacher, Lewis Hamilton, Juan Manuel Fangio, Alain Prost, Sebastian Vettel, Max Verstappen, Jack Brabham, Jackie Stewart, Niki Lauda, Nelson Piquet, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Wikipedia, List of Formula One World Drivers' Champions (1950–2025); Crash.net (2025 champion Lando Norris)

## Colours
Each bar takes the livery of the car the driver won his most recent title in, changing when he wins with a new team (e.g. Fangio: Alfa Romeo red, Mercedes silver 1954, Ferrari 1956, Maserati 1957; Schumacher: Benetton blue, then Ferrari red from 2000). Hamilton's 2020 title car was black; he is shown in Mercedes silver throughout 2014–2025 because a black bar disappears on the dark background (six of his seven titles were in silver cars). Drivers are hidden until their first title. The 2026 season is in progress and not counted.
