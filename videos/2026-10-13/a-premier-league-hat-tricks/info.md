# Who has scored the most Premier League hat-tricks?

## Title
Who has scored the most Premier League hat-tricks?

## Description
The race starts at the end of the first Premier League season, 1992-93, with Les Ferdinand out in front on two hat-tricks and Eric Cantona already in the books with the competition's very first one. Who has scored the most Premier League hat-tricks?

This race tracks every player's Premier League hat-tricks at the end of each season from 1992-93 to 2025-26. Alan Shearer ruled the 1990s and set the record of 11, Robbie Fowler, Michael Owen and Thierry Henry chased him through the 2000s, and Sergio Agüero finally took the record in 2020 before Erling Haaland's rapid rise.

For fans watching from Europe: two of the top seven are from mainland Europe, with France's Thierry Henry and Norway's Erling Haaland both on eight. Ruud van Nistelrooy and Robin van Persie (Netherlands) and Dimitar Berbatov (Bulgaria) sit just behind on five each.

Key Highlights:
Shearer's Record: Alan Shearer's 11th hat-trick came with five goals against Sheffield Wednesday on 19 September 1999, and the record stood for over 20 years.
Highbury Farewell: Thierry Henry hit a hat-trick against Wigan on 7 May 2006, the last game at Highbury, for his eighth.
Agüero Takes Over: Sergio Agüero equalled Shearer against Chelsea on 10 February 2019 and broke the record with his 12th at Aston Villa in January 2020.
Haaland's Rapid Rise: Erling Haaland reached eight hat-tricks in his first three Premier League seasons.

Data includes: Sergio Agüero, Alan Shearer, Robbie Fowler, Michael Owen, Thierry Henry, Harry Kane, Erling Haaland, Wayne Rooney, Luis Suárez, Andy Cole, Ian Wright, Ruud van Nistelrooy, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Wikipedia, List of Premier League hat-tricks (1992-93 to 2025-26); The Analyst (Opta), Premier League hat-tricks history.
