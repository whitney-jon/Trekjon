# Which NBA team has won the most games?

Style: **Standard** (`goals_race_vertical.py`, basketball theme) with the new record mode: each bar shows the franchise's all-time wins, with its all-time losses in a small line underneath. Team logos on the bars. Event cards sit in the centred position above the year.

## Description
For 22 years, the Boston Celtics were the winningest team in NBA history. Then Shaq and Kobe arrived. Which NBA team has won the most games?

This race tracks every franchise's all-time regular-season wins from 1980 to the end of the 2025-26 season, with each team's earlier history carried into the first frame. The Celtics start with a 160-win lead, but the Showtime Lakers chip away all through the 1980s, and in 2002 the Shaq-and-Kobe three-peat finally puts LA top. Boston wins it back in 2017 and finishes on 3,751 wins to the Lakers' 3,653.

Key Highlights:
Boston v LA: The Lakers led from 2002 to 2016; the Celtics have led every other year.
Best Record: The Lakers have the fewest losses of the top two (2,515).
Fast Climbers: OKC (from Seattle) and Phoenix both make the final top 10.
Losing Records: Knicks, Warriors, Hawks, Pistons and Kings all have more losses than wins.

Data includes: Boston Celtics, Los Angeles Lakers, Philadelphia 76ers, New York Knicks, Golden State Warriors, Atlanta Hawks, Detroit Pistons, Sacramento Kings, Oklahoma City Thunder, Phoenix Suns, and other historical top-ten franchises.

Data Source: Basketball-Reference franchise season-by-season records (NBA and BAA)

## How it works
- Regular-season wins and losses only (no playoffs), counted by franchise: e.g. the Lakers include Minneapolis, the Thunder include Seattle, the Kings include Rochester, Cincinnati and Kansas City, and the 76ers include Syracuse.
- BAA seasons (1946-49) count, because the NBA recognises them. ABA seasons do not count.
- Season years: 1980 = the 1979-80 season. The 2026-27 season is in progress and is not included.
- Each bar's big number is wins; the small line is losses (L).
