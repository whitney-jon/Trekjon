## Description
Since FIFA 10, EA has handed out its top rating every summer, and for a decade it went to Messi. Who is the highest-rated player in FIFA history?

This race shows the top 10 overall ratings in every edition from FIFA 10 to EA Sports FC 26. Messi starts on top, Cristiano Ronaldo takes No. 1 in FIFA 17 and 18, and then the Messi-Ronaldo era ends. Mbappé is No. 1 in FC 24, and Salah shares the top rating in FC 26.

Key Highlights:
Messi's Reign: Messi is No. 1 or joint No. 1 in 12 of the 14 FIFA editions from FIFA 10 to FIFA 23, with a peak of 94.
Ronaldo's Turn: Ronaldo is outright No. 1 in FIFA 17 and FIFA 18, on 94.
The New Era: Mbappé tops EA's list in FC 24 and FC 25 on 91, level with Haaland (and Rodri in FC 25).
FC 26: Salah and Mbappé share No. 1 on 91, with Dembélé, Van Dijk, Rodri, Bellingham and Haaland on 90.

Data includes: Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Neymar, Luis Suárez, Robert Lewandowski, Kevin De Bruyne, Kylian Mbappé, Mohamed Salah, Erling Haaland, Manuel Neuer, Eden Hazard and every other player who made an edition's top 10.

Data Source: EA's launch ratings for each edition, via GiveMeSport and SPORTbible (FIFA 10–20), FourFourTwo (FIFA 21), theScore (FIFA 22), SPORTbible (FIFA 23), GamesRadar (FC 24, FC 25) and Sports Illustrated (FC 26).

## How it works
- One frame per game: 2010 = FIFA 10 (released autumn 2009) and so on up to 2026 = EA Sports FC 26 (released September 2025). Ratings are launch-day base ratings, not in-season upgrades or special cards.
- Club badges show the player's club in that edition, e.g. Ronaldo moves from Real Madrid to Juventus in FIFA 19, Messi to PSG in FIFA 22 and to Inter Miami in FC 24.
- Players outside an edition's top 10 drop off the board. Their rating label holds their last top-10 value while they leave, so no rating that never existed is shown.
- Tied players are ordered as EA's own list orders them.
- FIFA 20 has 11 entries because ter Stegen and Neuer were both tenth on 90.

## QA
- FIFA 10–20: GiveMeSport and SPORTbible agree, except that SPORTbible lists Ronaldo at 89 in FIFA 13 (he was 92, which matches both GiveMeSport and Bleacher Report). Sources also disagree on Ribéry in FIFA 13 and FIFA 14 (89 or 90); GiveMeSport's 90 is used.
- FIFA 22: SPORTbible has Ronaldo 92 and Lewandowski 91. theScore's list of EA's reveal (Messi 93, Lewandowski 92, Ronaldo 91) is used.
- FC 24–26: these are the men's lists. FC 24 and later mix women's ratings into EA's reveal, and those are left out.
