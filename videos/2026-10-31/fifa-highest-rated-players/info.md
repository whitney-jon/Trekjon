## Description
Since FIFA 10, EA has handed out its top rating every summer, and for a decade it went to Messi. Who is the highest-rated player in FIFA history?

This race shows the top 10 overall ratings in every edition from FIFA 10 (2009) to EA Sports FC 27 (2026). Messi starts on top, Cristiano Ronaldo takes No. 1 in FIFA 17 and 18, and then the Messi-Ronaldo era ends. Mbappé is No. 1 in FC 24 and is back on top in FC 27, level with Haaland on 91.

Key Highlights:
Messi's Reign: Messi is No. 1 or joint No. 1 in 12 of the 14 FIFA editions from FIFA 10 to FIFA 23, with a peak of 94.
Ronaldo's Turn: Ronaldo is outright No. 1 in FIFA 17 and FIFA 18, on 94.
The Mbappé Era: Mbappé tops EA's list in FC 24, FC 25 and FC 27 on 91, sharing No. 1 with Salah in FC 26.
FC 27: Mbappé and Haaland lead on 91. Nine players are on 90, including newcomers Vitinha, Olise and Pedri.

Data includes: Kylian Mbappé, Erling Haaland, Rodri, Ousmane Dembélé, Vitinha, Harry Kane, Michael Olise, Pedri, Lamine Yamal, Thibaut Courtois, Jude Bellingham, Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo and every other player who made an edition's top 10.

Data Source: EA's launch ratings for each edition, via GiveMeSport and SPORTbible (FIFA 10–20), FourFourTwo (FIFA 21), theScore (FIFA 22), SPORTbible (FIFA 23), GamesRadar (FC 24, FC 25), Sports Illustrated (FC 26), and VGC and The Big Lead (FC 27).

## How it works
- One frame per game, placed in its release year: FIFA 10 came out in 2009 and EA Sports FC 27 in 2026. The on-screen label shows the game's name. Ratings are launch-day base ratings, not in-season upgrades or special cards.
- Club badges show the player's club in that edition, e.g. Ronaldo moves from Real Madrid to Juventus in FIFA 19, Messi to PSG in FIFA 22 and to Inter Miami in FC 24, and Salah drops out of the FC 27 top 10.
- Players outside an edition's top 10 drop off the board. Their rating label holds their last top-10 value while they leave, so no rating that never existed is shown.
- Tied players are ordered as EA's own list orders them. FIFA 20 and FC 27 have 11 entries because of ties at 90 for tenth place (FC 27: Courtois and Bellingham).

## QA
- FC 27 (released 2026): VGC (17 Aug 2026) and The Big Lead (18 Aug 2026) list the same top 27 men. Mbappé 91, Haaland 91, Rodri, Dembélé, Vitinha, Kane, Olise, Pedri, Yamal, Courtois and Bellingham 90. Salah is not in the top 27.
- FIFA 10–20: GiveMeSport and SPORTbible agree, except that SPORTbible lists Ronaldo at 89 in FIFA 13 (he was 92, which matches both GiveMeSport and Bleacher Report). Sources also disagree on Ribéry in FIFA 13 and FIFA 14 (89 or 90); GiveMeSport's 90 is used.
- FIFA 19: Messi and Ronaldo were both 94, and sources differ on which EA listed first. The video shows Messi first, so treat it as a joint No. 1.
- FIFA 22: SPORTbible has Ronaldo 92 and Lewandowski 91. theScore's list of EA's reveal (Messi 93, Lewandowski 92, Ronaldo 91) is used.
- FC 24 onwards: these are the men's lists. EA's reveals now mix in women's ratings, and those are left out.
