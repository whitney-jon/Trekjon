# Which NHL team has won the most games?

Style: **Standard** (`goals_race_vertical.py`, hockey theme) with record mode: each bar shows all-time wins, with losses (L) and ties (T) in a small line underneath. Team logos on the bars. Event cards sit in the centred position above the year.

## Description
The Montreal Canadiens have won more NHL games than anyone, and they've never let go of No. 1. But behind them, the Original Six have been fighting for 45 years. Which NHL team has won the most games?

This race tracks every franchise's all-time regular-season wins from 1979-80 to 2025-26, with each team's earlier history carried into the first frame. Montreal leads all the way to 3,644 wins. Boston jumps past Toronto for second in 1982 and stays there; Detroit passes the Leafs in 2009 before Toronto takes third place back in 2022.

Key Highlights:
Habs Forever: The Canadiens have been No. 1 in every season of the race.
Bruins Second: Boston passed Toronto in 1982 and now sits on 3,482 wins.
The Original Six: Montreal, Boston, Toronto, Detroit, the Rangers and Chicago are the top six.
The 1967 Teams: The Flyers, Blues, Penguins and Stars (North Stars) round out the top 10.

Data includes: Montreal Canadiens, Boston Bruins, Toronto Maple Leafs, Detroit Red Wings, New York Rangers, Chicago Blackhawks, Philadelphia Flyers, St. Louis Blues, Pittsburgh Penguins, Dallas Stars, and other historical top-ten franchises.

Data Source: Hockey-Reference franchise season-by-season records (NHL seasons only)

## How it works
- NHL regular-season games only (no playoffs, no NHA or WHA seasons). Franchises are counted as one team through moves and renames, e.g. Dallas includes the Minnesota North Stars and Calgary includes Atlanta.
- Losses (L) include overtime and shootout losses (since 1999-2000). Ties (T) ended after 2003-04, when the shootout was brought in, so tie totals stop growing.
- 2004-05 was cancelled by the lockout, so the bars stand still for one season.
- Season years: 1980 = 1979-80. The 2026-27 season is in progress and is not included.
