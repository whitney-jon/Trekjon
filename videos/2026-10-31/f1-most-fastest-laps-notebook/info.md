# Who has the most fastest laps in F1 history?

Style: **F1 Dark Notebook** (`pl_notebook_style.py`, `NB_PRESET=f1dark`, `--theme f1 --icon icons/f1-logo.png`, `--seconds 45`). It runs 42.6 s for 27 seasons, about 1.6 s per season, following the new slower pacing benchmark. Bars take the colours of each driver's team in that season. Team logos sit on the bars (no flags), and a logo appears only where the repo has one for that team (no Maserati, March, Ligier, Kurtis Kraft and similar). Retired drivers keep the team they set most of their fastest laps with. Event cards sit in the centred position above the year.

## Description
In 2000 Michael Schumacher drew level with Alain Prost's all-time record of 41 fastest laps. A year later it was his, and nobody has caught him since. Who has the most fastest laps in F1 history?

This race counts every fastest lap in a world championship Grand Prix, with all-time totals from 1950 carried into the 2000 starting frame, up to the 2026 Mexico City Grand Prix. Schumacher pulls away to 77, Kimi Räikkönen climbs past Prost, and Lewis Hamilton overtakes Räikkönen in 2019 to become the closest challenger. His fastest lap in Mexico on Sunday took him to 70.

Key Highlights:
Before the Race: Fangio, Clark and Prost all held the record before Schumacher. Their totals are in the opening frame.
Schumacher's 77: He passed Prost's 41 at the 2001 Australian Grand Prix and still holds the record.
Hamilton Closing In: Lewis Hamilton is second on 70 after his fastest lap in Mexico (1:19.268), with Kimi Räikkönen third on 46.

Data includes: Michael Schumacher, Lewis Hamilton, Kimi Räikkönen, Alain Prost, Max Verstappen, Sebastian Vettel, Nigel Mansell, Jim Clark, Fernando Alonso, Mika Häkkinen, Niki Lauda, Juan Manuel Fangio, Nelson Piquet and other drivers who reached the top 12.

Data Source: F1DB race-by-race fastest laps (github.com/f1db/f1db, release including the 2026 Mexico City GP), checked against Wikipedia's list of Formula One drivers who set a fastest lap and Autosport.

## How it works
- World championship Grands Prix only, including the Indianapolis 500 races that counted from 1950 to 1960. Sprint races are not included.
- Shared fastest laps count once for each driver who shared them, e.g. seven drivers shared the 1954 British GP fastest lap.
- 2026 runs to the Mexico City GP (round 19 of 23). Four races are still to come, so the 2026 figures may change.

## Events
- 2001 | Schumacher breaks Prost's record (Australian GP, 4 March 2001: Schumacher's 42nd).
- 2019 | Hamilton passes Räikkönen for No. 2 (Abu Dhabi GP, 1 December 2019: Hamilton's 47th, against Räikkönen's 46).
