## Description
When the Premier League began in 1992, half the league wore Umbro. For 33 seasons nobody could catch them. Which brand has made the most Premier League kits?

This race counts every Premier League club-season by kit supplier: one club wearing a brand for one season = one kit. It runs from 2000-01 to 2026-27, with the 1990s carried into the first frame. Umbro starts miles clear on 47. Reebok, Puma and Nike chase in the 2000s, and Adidas, kitting nine clubs in 2013-14, closes the gap. In 2025-26 Adidas finally pass Umbro, and with eight clubs this season they lead on 131 to Umbro's 121.

For UK fans: Umbro, the Manchester brand behind England's 1966 and Euro 96 kits, has dressed West Ham, Everton, Bournemouth, Brentford and Man City over the years.

Key Highlights:
Umbro's Head Start: 11 of 22 clubs wore Umbro in 1992-93.
Adidas Take Over: they lead from 2025-26, the first time anyone has passed Umbro.
Nike Third: 92 club-seasons, with Arsenal, Man United, Chelsea, Spurs, Brighton and Liverpool.
Gone But Not Forgotten: Pony, Asics, Ribero and Admiral all feature in the 90s.

Data includes: Adidas, Umbro, Nike, Puma, Reebok, Le Coq Sportif, Errea, Macron, Pony, Asics, and other kit brands.

Data Source: Kit Bliss (kitbliss.co.nz) English top-tier kit suppliers by season (1992-93 to 2024-25), checked against Wikipedia's Premier League season pages. For 2025-26 and 2026-27: Wikipedia (2025-26), Inside World Football and Footy Headlines (2026-27 brand counts), and Premier League club list.

## How it works
- One club wearing a brand for a Premier League season = 1. Totals from 1992-93 to 1999-2000 are carried into the first frame (Umbro 47, Pony 18, Puma 17, Asics 16, Adidas 15, Reebok 12, Nike 9).
- Year labels are seasons (2000-01 … 2026-27).
- A mid-season switch counts for the brand the club finished the season in (Crystal Palace 1992-93: Bukta then Ribero).
- Club own-brand kits (Southampton's "Saints", Coventry's own brand, Portsmouth's "Pompey Sport", WBA's "The Baggies") count under their own names and don't reach the top ten.
- 2026-27 is this season: Adidas 8 (Arsenal, Aston Villa, Fulham, Leeds, Liverpool, Man United, Newcastle, Nottingham Forest), Nike 3, Hummel 3, and Castore, Joma, Macron, Puma, Umbro and OXEN Sports 1 each.
