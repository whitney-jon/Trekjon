## Description
In 2006-07, Ryan Giggs already led the Premier League's all-time assists list with 125, well clear of Dennis Bergkamp on 94. Who has made the most Premier League assists?

This race tracks official Premier League assists, season by season, from 2006-07 to the end of 2025-26 (everything since 1992-93 is carried into the first frame). Giggs retires in 2014 on a record 162. Behind him, Frank Lampard climbs to second, Cesc Fàbregas and Wayne Rooney pass him, and in 2023-24 Kevin De Bruyne finishes the climb to No. 2. Mohamed Salah ends the race level with Bergkamp on 94.

For European fans: the top ten has a Welshman, a Belgian, two Spaniards, an Egyptian, a Dutchman and four Englishmen. De Bruyne and David Silva did it for Manchester City, Fàbregas and Bergkamp for Arsenal.

Key Highlights:
The Record: Ryan Giggs retired in 2014 with 162 assists, and nobody has come within 40.
The Chase for Second: Lampard, Fàbregas and De Bruyne have all held second place since 2012.
De Bruyne's Climb: he passed Fàbregas's 111 at Tottenham in May 2024 and finished on 119.
Salah Joins the Legends: his 18 assists in 2024-25 took him into the top ten, and he ends level with Bergkamp.

Data includes: Ryan Giggs, Kevin De Bruyne, Cesc Fàbregas, Wayne Rooney, Frank Lampard, Dennis Bergkamp, Mohamed Salah, David Silva, Steven Gerrard, James Milner, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Premier League official player stats, premierleague.com (1992-93 to 2025-26). Checked against The Analyst (Opta), NBC Sports and MyFootballFacts all-time tables.

## How the race works
- One time point per season end, labelled 2006-07 … 2025-26. Totals from 1992-93 to 2005-06 are carried into the first frame.
- Values are official Premier League assists (premierleague.com player pages: per-season figures and career totals).
- Badge and bar colour follow the player's Premier League club in each season (user's rules, 5 Nov 2026). Once a player leaves the Premier League he keeps his last PL club, except Lampard, who goes back to Chelsea after his 2014-15 season at Man City. So Fàbregas is Arsenal then Chelsea from 2014-15; Rooney Man United then Everton from 2017-18 (white bar so the blue Everton crest shows); Sheringham West Ham (real crest kept on the claret bar); Cole Portsmouth 2006-07, then Sunderland; Solano West Ham from 2007-08; Milner Newcastle → Aston Villa → Man City → Liverpool → Brighton; Eriksen Spurs → Brentford → Man United; De Bruyne Chelsea then Man City; Salah Chelsea then Liverpool. Legends who retired before 2006-07 show their main club (Bergkamp Arsenal, Beckham Man United, Shearer Newcastle, Anderton Spurs, Le Tissier Southampton, McManaman Liverpool).
