# Which is the richest football club in the world?

## Description
In 2001/02, Manchester United were the richest club in the world with €228m in revenue, ahead of Juventus and Bayern Munich. Today the top club earns more than five times that. Which is the richest football club in the world?

This race tracks the Deloitte Football Money League, the yearly ranking of clubs by revenue, from 2001/02 to 2024/25. United lead until Real Madrid take over in 2004/05. Real hold the top spot for 11 years, then United return, Barcelona lead in 2018/19 and 2019/20, Manchester City top it in 2020/21 and 2021/22, and Real Madrid become the first club past €1bn in 2023/24.

For European fans: Spain's big two lead the latest table, Real Madrid on €1.16bn and Barcelona on €975m, with Bayern Munich, PSG and six Premier League clubs completing the top ten.

Key Highlights:
United's Early Lead: Manchester United topped the first editions, from 1996/97 to 2003/04.
Real's Reign: Real Madrid were No. 1 for 11 straight seasons from 2004/05 to 2014/15.
City's Turn: Manchester City were the richest club in 2020/21 and 2021/22.
The €1bn Club: Real Madrid were the first club to pass €1bn (€1,045.5m in 2023/24), and reached €1,161m in 2024/25.

Data includes: Real Madrid, Barcelona, Bayern Munich, Paris Saint-Germain, Liverpool, Manchester City, Arsenal, Manchester United, Tottenham Hotspur, Chelsea, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Deloitte Football Money League (2003–2026 editions, covering 2001/02–2024/25), via Wikipedia; Goal.com (Money League 2026)

## How it works
- Each step is one season's revenue (not cumulative), as published in that year's Money League edition, in euros. Bars go up and down with each year's figure.
- Starts in 2001/02: Wikipedia's 2000/01 table has no figure for Leeds United (5th), so earlier seasons can't be shown accurately.
- A club outside that year's published top 20 has no figure, so its bar drops out until it returns (e.g. Man City before 2003/04, Newcastle in several seasons).
- 2019/20 and 2020/21 dip because of COVID-19 (no fans in stadiums).
- Bars use club colours and crests. Schalke 04 (10th in 2010/11) has no crest in our logo folder, so it shows colour only.
