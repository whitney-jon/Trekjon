# Who has played the most games in NBA history?

## Title
Who has played the most games in NBA history?

## Description
The race starts at the end of the 1991-92 NBA season, with Kareem Abdul-Jabbar out in front on 1,560 career games and Elvin Hayes second on 1,303. Who has played the most games in NBA history?

This race tracks career NBA regular-season games played at the end of every season from 1991-92 to 2025-26. Robert Parish takes the record from Kareem in 1996 and holds it for 30 years, while John Stockton, Karl Malone, Vince Carter and Dirk Nowitzki climb the list, before LeBron James takes the top spot in 2026.

For fans watching from Europe: Germany's Dirk Nowitzki is the only European in the final top ten, fifth on 1,522 games, all of them for the Dallas Mavericks.

Key Highlights:
The Chief Takes Over: Robert Parish played his 1,561st game on 6 April 1996, passing Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, and retired in 1997 on 1,611.
The Long Climb: Vince Carter passed Dirk Nowitzki for third in January 2020 and retired on 1,541 games.
LeBron's Run: LeBron James passed Kareem for second in April 2025 with his 1,561st game.
The New Record: LeBron broke Parish's record with game 1,612 on 21 March 2026 and finished the 2025-26 season on 1,622.

Data includes: LeBron James, Robert Parish, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Vince Carter, Dirk Nowitzki, John Stockton, Karl Malone, Kevin Garnett, Kevin Willis, Jason Terry, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Basketball-Reference (1991-92 to 2025-26); NBA.com.
