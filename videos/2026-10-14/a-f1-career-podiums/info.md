# Who has the most podium finishes in F1 history?

## Title
Who has the most podium finishes in F1 history?

## Description
The race starts at the end of the 1991 Formula 1 season, with Alain Prost out in front on 94 career podiums and Ayrton Senna second on 66. Who has the most podium finishes in F1 history?

This race tracks career Grand Prix podium finishes (top-three finishes, sprints not included) at the end of every season from 1991 to 2025. The record passes from Alain Prost to Michael Schumacher in 2002, then to Lewis Hamilton in 2020, while Max Verstappen climbs to third.

For fans watching from Europe: eight of the final top ten are European, led by Britain's Lewis Hamilton, Germany's Michael Schumacher and the Netherlands' Max Verstappen. Brazil's Ayrton Senna and Rubens Barrichello are the only non-Europeans in the top ten.

Key Highlights:
The Prost Benchmark: Alain Prost retired at the end of 1993 with 106 podiums, the record at the time.
The Schumacher Era: Michael Schumacher passed Prost in 2002, a season in which he finished on the podium in all 17 races, and ended his career on 155.
Hamilton Takes Over: Lewis Hamilton broke Schumacher's record of 155 with his 156th podium at the 2020 Spanish Grand Prix.
The Final Mark: Hamilton finished 2025 on 202 podiums, with Verstappen third on 127 after passing Sebastian Vettel's 122.

Data includes: Lewis Hamilton, Michael Schumacher, Max Verstappen, Sebastian Vettel, Alain Prost, Fernando Alonso, Kimi Räikkönen, Ayrton Senna, Rubens Barrichello, Valtteri Bottas, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: pitwall.app season-by-season driver statistics (1991–2025); RacingNews365 and Formula1.com career statistics.

## Event cards
- 2002: Schumacher breaks Prost's record of 106 podiums. His 100th podium was the 2002 Brazilian GP (Motorsport.com, "Flash back to Schumacher's 100th F1 podium"); he was on the podium in every 2002 race, so the 107th came at the British GP on 7 Jul 2002. Reuters/Atlas F1 (11 Oct 2002) reports Schumacher took the career podium record in July 2002. CSV: 97 at end of 2001, 114 at end of 2002, against Prost's 106.
- 2020: Hamilton breaks Schumacher's record of 155. He equalled 155 at the 70th Anniversary GP (9 Aug 2020) and took his 156th at the Spanish GP (16 Aug 2020) (Formula1.com). CSV: 151 at end of 2019, 165 at end of 2020.

- Alonso and Prost are level on 106 at the end; the bar order keeps Prost (who got there first) above Alonso.
