# Who has won the most English league titles?

## Title
Who has won the most English league titles?

## Description
The race starts in 1992, at the end of the last First Division season before the Premier League, with Liverpool on 18 English league titles and Arsenal next on 10. Who has won the most English top-flight titles?

This race tracks every club's all-time English top-flight titles (First Division and Premier League, counted from 1888–89) at the end of every season from 1991–92 to 2025–26. Manchester United go from 7 titles to 20, Manchester City climb from 2 to 10, and Liverpool end the race level with United on 20.

For fans watching from Europe: this is England's domestic league record, so every club here is English. It ends in a dead heat between the two most successful clubs in English league history, Manchester United and Liverpool, on 20 titles each.

Key Highlights:
The Ferguson Era: Manchester United won 13 titles between 1993 and 2013, going from 7 to 20.
The Record Falls: United's 18th title in 2009 equalled Liverpool's record, and their 19th in 2011 took them clear.
City's Rise: Manchester City won eight titles from 2012 to 2024, climbing from 2 to 10.
Level at the Top: Liverpool won the league in 2020 and 2025 to draw level on 20, and Arsenal's 2026 title was their 14th.

Data includes: Manchester United, Liverpool, Arsenal, Manchester City, Everton, Aston Villa, Sunderland, Chelsea, Newcastle United, Sheffield Wednesday, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Wikipedia, List of English football champions (1888–89 to 2025–26); The Football Faithful, "The English clubs with the most top-flight title wins" (June 2026).
