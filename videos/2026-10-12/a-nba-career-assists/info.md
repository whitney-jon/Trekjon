# Who has the most assists in NBA history?

## Title
Who has the most assists in NBA history?

## Description
The race starts at the end of the 1989-90 NBA season, with Oscar Robertson holding the record on 9,887 career assists and Magic Johnson closing in on 8,932. Who has the most assists in NBA history?

This race tracks career regular-season assists at the end of every season from 1989-90 to 2025-26. The record passes from Robertson to Magic Johnson and then to John Stockton, while Jason Kidd, Chris Paul and LeBron James fight over the places behind him.

For fans watching from Europe: no European player makes the final top ten. The only non-American is Canada's Steve Nash, sixth on 10,335, just one assist ahead of Mark Jackson.

Key Highlights:
Magic Takes the Record: Magic Johnson passed Oscar Robertson's 9,887 on 15 April 1991 against Dallas.
Stockton's Reign: John Stockton passed Magic on 1 February 1995 and retired in 2003 with 15,806, still the record.
The Race for Second: Chris Paul passed Jason Kidd for second place on 8 December 2024 and finished on 12,552.
One Assist Apart: Steve Nash (10,335) and Mark Jackson (10,334) end the race separated by a single assist.

Data includes: John Stockton, Chris Paul, Jason Kidd, LeBron James, Russell Westbrook, Steve Nash, Mark Jackson, Magic Johnson, Oscar Robertson, Isiah Thomas, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Basketball-Reference (1989-90 to 2025-26); Land of Basketball season-by-season totals.
