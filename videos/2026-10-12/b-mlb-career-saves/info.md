# Who has the most saves in MLB history?

## Title
Who has the most saves in MLB history?

## Description
The race starts at the end of the 1991 MLB season, with Rollie Fingers holding the record on 341 career saves and Jeff Reardon 14 behind on 327. Who has the most saves in MLB history?

This race tracks career regular-season saves at the end of every season from 1991 to 2026. The record changes hands four times: from Fingers to Reardon, then Lee Smith, Trevor Hoffman and finally Mariano Rivera, while Kenley Jansen, Craig Kimbrel and Aroldis Chapman climb the list in the 2020s.

For fans watching from Europe: no European-born pitcher makes the final top ten, but Kenley Jansen, third on 498, plays for the Kingdom of the Netherlands at the World Baseball Classic. The top ten also includes Panama's Mariano Rivera, Venezuela's Francisco Rodríguez and Cuba's Aroldis Chapman.

Key Highlights:
Four Record Holders: Jeff Reardon passed Rollie Fingers on 15 June 1992, and Lee Smith passed Reardon on 13 April 1993.
Hoffman's Turn: Trevor Hoffman broke Lee Smith's record with save No. 479 on 24 September 2006.
Mo Takes Over: Mariano Rivera's 602nd save on 19 September 2011 against the Twins made him the all-time leader, and he retired on 652.
Jansen Climbs: Kenley Jansen passed Lee Smith for third in April 2026 and finished the season on 498.

Data includes: Mariano Rivera, Trevor Hoffman, Kenley Jansen, Lee Smith, Craig Kimbrel, Francisco Rodríguez, John Franco, Billy Wagner, Aroldis Chapman, Dennis Eckersley, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Baseball-Reference (1991–2026); MLB.com career saves leaders (Sept 2026).
