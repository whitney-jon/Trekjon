# What is the most valuable sports team in the world?

## Description
In 2010, Manchester United topped Forbes' first list of the world's most valuable sports teams at $1.83 billion. Fifteen years later, the No. 1 team is worth seven times that. What is the most valuable sports team in the world?

This race follows every Forbes list of the world's most valuable sports teams from 2010 to 2025. Manchester United lead the first three years, Real Madrid take over from 2013 to 2015, and in 2016 the Dallas Cowboys become the first team worth $4 billion and go to No. 1, where they have stayed ever since, reaching $13 billion in 2025. Behind them, the NFL, the New York Yankees and NBA teams like the Golden State Warriors and Los Angeles Lakers push Europe's football giants down the list.

For European fans: Real Madrid, Barcelona and Manchester United were all in the top five as recently as 2018. In the 2025 list, Real Madrid ($6.75bn) and Manchester United ($6.6bn) are the highest football clubs, in 20th and 24th.

Key Highlights:
United's Start: Manchester United were No. 1 in 2010, 2011 and 2012.
Real's Reign: Real Madrid led from 2013 to 2015.
America's Team: The Dallas Cowboys have been No. 1 every year since 2016, rising from $4 billion to $13 billion.
NBA Surge: The Golden State Warriors ($11bn) and Los Angeles Lakers ($10bn) are second and fifth in 2025.

Data includes: Dallas Cowboys, Golden State Warriors, Los Angeles Rams, New York Giants, Los Angeles Lakers, New York Knicks, New England Patriots, San Francisco 49ers, Philadelphia Eagles, New York Yankees, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Forbes, The World's Most Valuable Sports Teams (2010–2025), via Wikipedia; Forbes Australia (2024 and 2025 lists); theScore (2024); El Economista (2023)

## How it works
- One step per Forbes list (2010 to 2025, labelled by list year). Values are Forbes' valuations in US dollars, not cumulative.
- Forbes has not yet published a 2026 overall list (its 2025 list came out on 19 December 2025), so the race ends at 2025.
- The 2010–2016 lists are only available as a top 10, so a team that drops out of the top 10 in those years has no value and leaves the chart. From 2017 the top 50 is known.
- Bars use team colours and country flags (USA, Spain, England, Germany). There are no NFL/MLB logos in our folder, so no crests are used.
- Washington's name changes on screen: Redskins until 2019, Washington Football Team 2020–21, Commanders from 2022.

## Events

- 2016 | Dallas Cowboys take No. 1 ($4bn), a spot they still hold.

## QA
- 2025 top 25 matches Forbes Australia (19 Dec 2025) exactly; 2023 top 15 matches El Economista (20 Sep 2023).
- 2024: Wikipedia lists the New England Patriots 2nd at $9bn. Forbes Australia's copy of the official list (13 Dec 2024) and theScore both show $7.4bn, 6th, so the video uses $7.4bn. All other 2024 values matched.
- 2022: Wikipedia shows Washington at $6.05bn while ranking it 8th, tied with the Warriors at $5.6bn; Front Office Sports (9 Sep 2022) gives $5.6bn, which is used.
- 2019: Fox News gives Barcelona $4.2bn; Forbes' 2019 football valuations (and Wikipedia) give $4.02bn, which is used.
- Tied teams keep the same value (e.g. 2025 Bears and Yankees, both $8.2bn).
