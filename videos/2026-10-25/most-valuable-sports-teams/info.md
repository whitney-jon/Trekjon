# What is the most valuable sports team in the world?

## Description
In 2010, Manchester United topped Forbes' first list of the world's most valuable sports teams at $1.83 billion. Today the No. 1 team is worth more than nine times that. What is the most valuable sports team in the world?

This race follows every Forbes list of the world's most valuable sports teams from 2010 to 2025, plus Forbes' latest 2026 valuations. Manchester United lead the first three years, Real Madrid take over from 2013 to 2015, and in 2016 the Dallas Cowboys become the first team worth $4 billion and go to No. 1, where they have stayed ever since, reaching $17 billion in 2026. Behind them, the NFL, the New York Yankees and NBA teams like the Golden State Warriors and Los Angeles Lakers push Europe's football giants down the list.

For European fans: Real Madrid, Barcelona and Manchester United were all in the top five as recently as 2018. In the 2025 list, Real Madrid ($6.75bn) and Manchester United ($6.6bn) are the highest football clubs, in 20th and 24th.

Key Highlights:
United's Start: Manchester United were No. 1 in 2010, 2011 and 2012.
Real's Reign: Real Madrid led from 2013 to 2015.
America's Team: The Dallas Cowboys have been No. 1 every year since 2016, rising from $4 billion to $13 billion.
NBA Surge: In 2026 the Golden State Warriors ($13.5bn, level with the Rams), New York Knicks ($12.6bn) and Los Angeles Lakers ($12.5bn) are all in the top five.

Data includes: Dallas Cowboys, Golden State Warriors, Los Angeles Rams, New York Giants, Los Angeles Lakers, New York Knicks, New England Patriots, San Francisco 49ers, Philadelphia Eagles, New York Yankees, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Forbes, The World's Most Valuable Sports Teams (2010–2025), via Wikipedia; Forbes Australia (2024 and 2025 lists); theScore (2024); El Economista (2023). 2026: Forbes NFL valuations (Forbes Australia, 10 Sep 2026), NBA valuations (Forbes Italia, 17 Oct 2026; Forbes Argentina, 19 Oct 2026), MLB valuations (Patch and Legit.ng citing Forbes, Mar 2026), soccer valuations (Sports Illustrated and World Soccer Talk citing Forbes, 29 May 2026)

## How it works
- One step per Forbes list (2010 to 2025, labelled by list year). Values are Forbes' valuations in US dollars, not cumulative.
- Forbes has not yet published its 2026 overall list (usually December). The 2026 step uses Forbes' own 2026 league valuations instead, which feed that list: NFL (Sep 2026), NBA (Oct 2026), MLB (Mar 2026) and soccer (May 2026). Re-render with the official overall list when it comes out.
- The 2010–2016 lists are only available as a top 10, so a team that drops out of the top 10 in those years has no value and leaves the chart. From 2017 the top 50 is known.
- Bars use team colours with each team's logo (logos replace the country flags). Washington's logo changes with its name: Redskins until 2019, Washington Football Team 2020–21, Commanders from 2022. Raiders use the Las Vegas Raiders logo.
- Washington's name changes on screen: Redskins until 2019, Washington Football Team 2020–21, Commanders from 2022.
