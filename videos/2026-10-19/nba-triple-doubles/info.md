# Who has the most triple-doubles in NBA history?

## Description
At the turn of the century, Oscar Robertson's 181 career triple-doubles had stood as the NBA record since 1974, with Magic Johnson a distant second on 138. Then came Russell Westbrook and Nikola Jokić. Who has the most triple-doubles in NBA history?

This race tracks career regular-season triple-doubles from 1999-2000 to the end of the 2025-26 season, with each player's MVP trophies counted beside his total. Jason Kidd and LeBron James climb steadily, Westbrook averages a triple-double for a season and wins the 2017 MVP, then breaks Oscar's record in 2021. Jokić, a three-time MVP, passes Oscar in February 2026 and starts the new season 11 behind Westbrook.

For European fans: Serbia's Nikola Jokić and Slovenia's Luka Dončić are both in the all-time top ten, and Jokić is the man chasing the record.

Key Highlights:
The Big O's Record: Oscar Robertson's 181 triple-doubles stood as the record for 47 years.
Westbrook's Season: Russell Westbrook's record 42 triple-doubles in 2016-17 won him the MVP.
A New King: Westbrook passed Robertson in May 2021 and retired in 2026 on 209.
The Chase: Jokić has 198 and three MVPs; at his recent pace of 34 a season he could pass Westbrook this season.

Data includes: Russell Westbrook, Nikola Jokić, Oscar Robertson, Magic Johnson, LeBron James, Jason Kidd, Luka Dončić, James Harden, Wilt Chamberlain, Domantas Sabonis, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Basketball-Reference (single-season triple-double leaders; player Totals); Land of Basketball (triple-doubles by season); NBA.com all-time triple-double leaders (Aug 2026)
