# Who has the most receiving yards in NFL history?

## Description
In 1986, Charlie Joiner's 12,146 career receiving yards led the NFL's all-time list, with Don Maynard and Steve Largent close behind. Then came Jerry Rice. Who has the most receiving yards in NFL history?

This race tracks career regular-season receiving yards from 1986 to 2020, with each bar in the colours of the team the player was on that season. Steve Largent and James Lofton take turns at the top, Rice passes Lofton in 1995 and keeps going all the way to 22,895, and Terrell Owens, Randy Moss and Larry Fitzgerald battle for second, which Fitzgerald takes in 2018.

For European fans: these are the record-holders behind the game the NFL now takes to London, Germany, Dublin and Madrid every season.

Key Highlights:
Rice's Record: Jerry Rice broke James Lofton's career record in 1995 and retired in 2004 with 22,895 yards.
The Gap: Rice is more than 5,400 yards ahead of anyone else.
Fitzgerald's Climb: Larry Fitzgerald passed Terrell Owens for second in 2018 and finished on 17,492, all with the Arizona Cardinals.
The Pack: Owens, Moss, Bruce, Gonzalez, Brown, Smith, Harrison and Wayne are separated by fewer than 1,600 yards.

Data includes: Jerry Rice, Larry Fitzgerald, Terrell Owens, Randy Moss, Isaac Bruce, Tony Gonzalez, Tim Brown, Steve Smith Sr., Marvin Harrison, Reggie Wayne, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Pro Football Reference (player season stats; career receiving yards leaders through each season, 1986–2020); NFL.com and Wikipedia (Terrell Owens)

## Team colours
Each bar takes the colours of the player's team that season and changes when he moves (e.g. Jerry Rice: 49ers red and gold, Raiders black and silver 2001–04, Seahawks 2004; Terrell Owens: 49ers, Eagles 2004, Cowboys 2006, Bills 2009, Bengals 2010; Randy Moss: Vikings, Raiders 2005, Patriots 2007, Titans 2010, 49ers 2012). Old liveries where they differ: Eagles Kelly green before 1996, Patriots red before 1993, Houston Oilers. Before a player's first season his first team's colours are used; after he retires, the team he played most seasons for. Players retired before 1986 (Joiner, Maynard, Jackson, Alworth, Berry, Taylor, Carmichael, Biletnikoff) keep their main team's colours.
