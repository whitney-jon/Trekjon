# Which country has won the most marathons?

## Description
For almost a century, Americans won the Boston Marathon more than anyone else. Then Kenya arrived. Which country has won the most marathons?

This race counts every men's and women's win at the six World Marathon Majors (Boston, New York, Chicago, Berlin, London and Tokyo), with each country's all-time total carried into 1980. The USA starts miles ahead thanks to Boston, while Germany, Britain and Norway build their tallies. Kenya's first major win comes in 1983; by 2000 they are second, and in 2013 they pass the USA. Today Kenya has 189 wins, almost double the USA's 105, with Ethiopia third on 81.

For UK fans: Great Britain's 36 wins put them joint fourth with Germany, thanks to London champions like Paula Radcliffe and Chicago wins from Steve Jones and Paul Evans.

Key Highlights:
Boston's Legacy: The USA's early lead comes from more than 100 years of Boston Marathons.
Kenya's Takeover: Kenya overtook the USA in 2013 and has added about 7 wins a year since.
Ethiopia's Rise: Ethiopia went from 11 wins in 2006 to 81 today.
Norway's Queens: 19 of Norway's 20 wins came in the women's race, led by Grete Waitz's nine New York titles.

Data includes: Kenya, USA, Ethiopia, Germany, Great Britain, Japan, Norway, Canada, Finland, Portugal, and other historical top-ten countries.

Data Source: Wikipedia lists of winners of the Boston, New York City, Chicago, Berlin, London and Tokyo marathons; 2026 Berlin and Tokyo results (World Athletics, race reports)

## How it works
- Men's and women's open (elite) race winners at the six World Marathon Majors; wheelchair and handcycle races are not counted.
- Boston from 1897 (women from 1966, as recognised by the BAA), New York from 1970, Berlin 1974, Chicago 1977, London 1981, Tokyo from 2007 (when it became the Tokyo Marathon). Totals before 1980 are carried into the first frame (USA 79, Germany 16, Canada 16).
- Cancelled races are skipped (Boston 2020, New York 2012 and 2020, Berlin 2020, Chicago 1987 and 2020; NYC 2020 virtual "winners" are not counted). Tokyo's 2021 edition, run in March 2022, counts in 2022.
- London 1981's men's dead heat counts for both the USA and Norway.
- West and East German wins are counted as Germany.
- 2026 includes Tokyo (Takele ETH, Kosgei KEN), Boston, London, Berlin (Adola ETH, Assefa ETH) and Chicago. New York 2026 (1 November) is not included yet.
