Who has scored the most since 2020?

## How it works
- Goals for club (all competitions) plus goals for country, season by season from 2020-21; "Now" = 2026-27 so far (to 30 October 2026).
- Club seasons come from each player's club statistics table; international goals are dated and grouped into seasons running August to July (so the June–July 2026 World Cup counts in 2025-26).
- Club goals: Mbappé 42, 39, 41, 44, 44, 42, 14; Haaland 41, 29, 52, 38, 34, 38, 13; Kane 33, 27, 32, 44, 41, 61, 10; Salah 31, 31, 30, 25, 34, 12, 10.
- International goals: Mbappé 4, 10, 13, 8, 2, 16, 1; Haaland 7, 13, 4, 7, 11, 20, 3; Kane 6, 12, 8, 8, 7, 12, 6; Salah 2, 2, 4, 6, 3, 8, 0. Totals match each player's Wikipedia international goals tally (Mbappé 67, Haaland 65, Kane 91, Salah 68).
- Golden Boots = league top-scorer awards (Premier League Golden Boot, Ligue 1 top scorer, Pichichi, Bundesliga top scorer), European Golden Shoe, World Cup Golden Boot and Euro top scorer, from 2020-21:
  - Mbappé: Ligue 1 2020-21, 21-22, 22-23, 23-24; 2022 World Cup; Pichichi 2024-25, 25-26; Golden Shoe 2024-25; 2026 World Cup.
  - Haaland: Premier League 2022-23, 23-24, 25-26; Golden Shoe 2022-23.
  - Kane: Premier League 2020-21; Bundesliga 2023-24, 24-25, 25-26; Golden Shoe 2023-24, 25-26; Euro 2024 (shared).
  - Salah: Premier League 2021-22 (shared), 2024-25.
  - Ballon d'Or: Kane, 2026 (26 October 2026).
- Bars show each player's club logo and colours for the season (Haaland: Dortmund then City; Mbappé: PSG then Real Madrid; Kane: Spurs then Bayern; Salah: Liverpool then Trabzonspor).
- Salah's Trabzonspor bar uses the club's light blue so the claret crest stays visible.
