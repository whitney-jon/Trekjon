## Description
Liverpool or Manchester United? For more than 30 years they have traded the title of England's most successful club. Which club has won the most trophies in English football?

This race counts every major trophy won by English clubs, from the league and FA Cup to the Community Shield and European cups, starting with each club's all-time total in 1991. Liverpool begin with a 21-trophy lead, but Sir Alex Ferguson's Manchester United catch them in 2011 and go top in 2013. Then Liverpool's 2022 cup double and Community Shield put them back in front, and they finish on 70 to United's 68. Behind them, Manchester City and Chelsea race up from the pack.

Key Highlights:
The Ferguson Effect: United won 41 trophies from 1992 to 2026 to overtake Liverpool.
Liverpool's Return: Liverpool retook No. 1 in 2022 and lead 70–68.
Arsenal Third: Arsenal's 2026 title and Community Shield take them to 51.
The New Money: Man City (12 → 38) and Chelsea (7 → 36) climb past Spurs, Villa and Everton.

Data includes: Liverpool, Manchester United, Arsenal, Manchester City, Chelsea, Tottenham Hotspur, Aston Villa, Everton, Newcastle United, Nottingham Forest, and other historical top-ten clubs.

Data Source: Wikipedia, List of football clubs in England by competitive honours won (updated 16 Aug 2026), plus the lists of English champions, FA Cup, EFL Cup and Community Shield finals, and English clubs in international competitions

## How it works
- Counts every competitive trophy in Wikipedia's honours list: league titles, FA Cup, League Cup, Community Shield (incl. Charity Shield; shared shields count for both clubs), European Cup/Champions League, UEFA Cup/Europa League, Conference League, Cup Winners' Cup, UEFA Super Cup, Club World Cup/Intercontinental Cup, Inter-Cities Fairs Cup, and the "other" honours Wikipedia counts (Full Members' Cup, Football League Super Cup, Centenary Trophy, Anglo-Italian League Cup, Intertoto Cup, Sheriff of London Charity Shield).
- Years are calendar years: a trophy counts in the year it was won (Community Shields and Super Cups in August, Club World Cups when played). 2026 includes Arsenal's league title and Community Shield, Man City's FA Cup and League Cup, and Aston Villa's Europa League.
- 1991 totals = each club's all-time total minus its trophies from 1992 onwards.
- Everton's bar is white with a blue edge so the blue crest stays visible.
- Icons: League (Premier League trophy), FA Cup, League Cup, Shield, Europe (Champions League icon, also used for the Super Cup), Europa (Europa League icon, also used for the Conference League, Cup Winners' Cup and Intertoto Cup), World (Club World Cup). The FA Cup and League Cup icons are simple drawn silhouettes in the same style as the uploaded icons.
