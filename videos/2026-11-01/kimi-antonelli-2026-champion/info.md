# How did Kimi Antonelli become F1 champion?

## Description
George Russell won the 2026 opener in Australia. By the third race, his 19-year-old Mercedes team-mate had taken the lead, and he never gave it back. How did Kimi Antonelli become F1 champion?

This race shows the 2026 drivers' championship points after every Grand Prix, from Australia to Mexico. Antonelli takes the lead in Japan and pulls away, while Russell, Hamilton, Verstappen and Leclerc swap places behind him all season. Third place in Mexico City put him 119 points clear with only 100 left to win, which made him the youngest world champion in F1 history.

Key Highlights:
Early Take-over: Antonelli trailed Russell after Australia and China, then led from Japan onwards.
Runaway Lead: 156 points after Monaco, 292 after Madrid, 380 after Mexico.
The Fight Behind: Verstappen (261), Hamilton (259), Russell (250) and Leclerc (245) are covered by 16 points for second.
Champion in Mexico: Verstappen won, Hamilton was second, and Antonelli's third place sealed the title with four races to spare.

Data includes: Kimi Antonelli, Max Verstappen, Lewis Hamilton, George Russell, Charles Leclerc, Lando Norris, Oscar Piastri, Isack Hadjar, Liam Lawson, Pierre Gasly and other 2026 drivers.

Data Source: F1DB championship standings after each 2026 round (github.com/f1db/f1db), checked against GPblog's standings before Mexico. Mexico City GP result from The Race (full classification), Autosport and PlanetF1 (top four and title).
