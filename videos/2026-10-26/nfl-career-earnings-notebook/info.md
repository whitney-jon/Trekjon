# Who has earned the most in NFL history?

Style: **NFL Notebook** (Notebook hand-drawn skin with an NFL navy header and red underline), rendered with `pl_notebook_style.py` and `NB_PRESET=nfl`. Team logos replace flags and change as players move teams.

## Description
In 1996, John Elway was the highest-paid player in NFL history, with $37.8 million in career earnings. Today the record is more than ten times that. Who has earned the most in NFL history?

This race tracks NFL career cash earnings season by season from 1996 to 2025, with each bar in the colours and logo of the player's team that season. Elway and Dan Marino lead the 1990s, Troy Aikman takes over in 1999 and Drew Bledsoe in 2002, before Peyton Manning's $35 million 2004 season puts him top for 15 years. Eli Manning passes his brother in 2019, then Drew Brees, Tom Brady and Aaron Rodgers each take a turn at No. 1, until Matthew Stafford passes Rodgers in 2025 to finish on $408.3 million.

For European fans: these are the quarterbacks behind the NFL's international games in London, Germany, Dublin and Madrid. Every leader since 1996 has been a quarterback.

Key Highlights:
Peyton's Reign: Peyton Manning was the NFL's top career earner from 2004 to 2018.
Brother v Brother: Eli Manning passed Peyton in 2019 ($252.3m v $248.7m).
The $300m Club: Brady, Rodgers, Stafford, Cousins, Wilson and Ryan have all passed $300 million.
Stafford's Record: Matthew Stafford passed Aaron Rodgers in 2025 and leads on $408.3 million.

Data includes: Matthew Stafford, Aaron Rodgers, Tom Brady, Kirk Cousins, Russell Wilson, Matt Ryan, Dak Prescott, Drew Brees, Ben Roethlisberger, Peyton Manning, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: Spotrac, NFL player career earnings (cash by season, 1979–2025) and season cash rankings (1990–2025)

## How it works
- Values are career cash earnings added up season by season: salary, signing and roster bonuses and guarantees in the year they were paid (Spotrac's "cash" figure). Endorsements are not included.
- Ends with the 2025 season. The 2026 season in progress is not included, so totals are lower than Spotrac's current career figures (e.g. Stafford $408.3m here, $448.3m on Spotrac including 2026).
- Each step is one NFL season (labelled by the year it started). Earnings before 1996 are carried into the first frame (e.g. Elway $37.8m, Marino $36.3m).
- Bars and logos follow the team that paid the player each season (the active team, not a team still owing dead money). Retired players keep their last team.
- Logos change with the franchise: Rams horn logo until 2019 and LA logo from 2020; Jets logo change in 2024; Raiders (Oakland/Las Vegas).
- One-colour logos that match their bar colour (Colts horseshoe, Giants "ny") are shown in white so they stay visible.
- Players were chosen from Spotrac's season cash rankings for every year 1990–2025 (top 16 cumulative earners at any point), then each player's full earnings history was pulled.
