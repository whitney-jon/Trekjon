# Who has earned the most in PGA Tour history?

Style: **Golf Notebook** (Notebook hand-drawn skin with a green golf header), rendered with `pl_notebook_style.py` and `NB_PRESET=golf`.

## Description
When Tiger Woods turned pro in 1996, Greg Norman was golf's all-time money leader on just over $10 million. Thirty years later, the record is more than ten times that. Who has earned the most in PGA Tour history?

This race tracks official PGA Tour career earnings from 1996 to October 2026. Norman, Davis Love III and Nick Price lead the 1990s, then Tiger takes the record in 2000 and holds it for 26 years, reaching $121 million. Phil Mickelson and Vijay Singh chase him through the 2000s, Rory McIlroy climbs to second in 2025, and Scottie Scheffler's record-breaking seasons take him past Tiger at the 2026 Tour Championship, to $130.4 million.

For European fans: Northern Ireland's Rory McIlroy ($119.6m) is third all-time, and England's Justin Rose ($79.4m) is fifth.

Key Highlights:
Tiger's Takeover: Tiger earned $9.2m in 2000 and went from fourth to first on the all-time list in a single season.
The Chasers: Phil Mickelson ($96.7m) and Vijay Singh ($71.3m) spent years behind Tiger.
Rory's Rise: McIlroy passed Mickelson for second in 2025.
Scheffler's Record: Scheffler's $10m Tour Championship win in August 2026 took him past Tiger's $120,999,166; his $30.9m in 2026 is the most ever in one PGA Tour season.

Data includes: Scottie Scheffler, Tiger Woods, Rory McIlroy, Phil Mickelson, Justin Rose, Dustin Johnson, Adam Scott, Justin Thomas, Jim Furyk, Vijay Singh, and other historical top-ten contenders.

Data Source: PGA Tour official money by season (1987–2026), non-member earnings by season (2000–2026) and career money leaders (pgatour.com, through week ending 19 Oct 2026); Golf Channel (30 Aug 2026)

## How it works
- Official PGA Tour earnings (official money plus non-member earnings, which the PGA Tour counts in career money), added up season by season. Split seasons (2013-14 to 2022-23) count in the year they ended.
- Players who started before 1987 get their earlier earnings as a starting value (career total minus 1987–2026 seasons), e.g. Tom Kite $2.92m, Hal Sutton $1.69m, Greg Norman $1.28m.
- Players who left for LIV (Mickelson, Dustin Johnson, García) are kept with their PGA Tour earnings, though the PGA Tour's current career list no longer shows them.
- The 2026 season is counted through the week ending 19 October 2026; a few fall events remain.
- Bars use a highlighter palette, as most players are American; flags show nationality (Norman, Scott, Day AU; Price ZW; Singh FJ; Els ZA; García ES; Weir CA; Rose England; McIlroy Northern Ireland).
