## Description
In 2000 Rangers led Celtic by 22 trophies, 100 to 78. Which club has won the most trophies in Scottish football?

This race counts every major Scottish honour (league titles, Scottish Cups, League Cups and European trophies), starting from each club's all-time total at the end of 2000. Rangers stall on 115 from 2011 to 2020, while Celtic's nine-in-a-row and back-to-back trebles close the gap. In December 2024 Celtic's League Cup win takes them past Rangers, 119 to 118. It is the first time they have led since 1938.

For the European fan: the totals include Celtic's 1967 European Cup, Rangers' 1972 Cup Winners' Cup, and Aberdeen's 1983 Cup Winners' Cup and Super Cup under Alex Ferguson.
