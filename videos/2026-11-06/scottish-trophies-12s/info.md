## Description
In 2000 Rangers led Celtic by 22 trophies, 100 to 78. Which club has won the most trophies in Scottish football?

This race counts every Scottish league title from the top flight down to League Two, plus Scottish Cups, League Cups and European trophies, starting from each club's all-time total at the end of 2000. Rangers' climb back through the leagues (Third Division 2013, League One 2014, Championship 2016) keeps them ahead. Celtic's nine-in-a-row and back-to-back trebles close the gap to 121–120 in 2025. Then the 2026 league and Scottish Cup double takes Celtic top, 122 to 121. It is the first time they have led since 1938.

For the European fan: the totals include Celtic's 1967 European Cup, Rangers' 1972 Cup Winners' Cup, and Aberdeen's 1983 Cup Winners' Cup and Super Cup under Alex Ferguson.

## How it counts
- **Trophies:** top-flight league titles, Scottish Cups, League Cups, European trophies (Celtic 1967, Rangers 1972, Aberdeen 1983 ×2), **plus league titles in tiers 2–4.** Tier 2: Division Two / First Division / Championship. Tier 3: Division Three 1923–26, Division C first teams 1946–55, Second Division / League One. Tier 4: Third Division / League Two. Reserve ("A") teams' Division C wins are left out, and shared titles (1910) count for both clubs. - **Calendar years**, as in the English trophies video: a trophy counts in the year it was lifted. League Cup finals played in November or December count in that year.
